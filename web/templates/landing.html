How it works

Three steps. No app. Just WhatsApp.

Built around how Kenyan landlords and tenants already use M-Pesa — not how a Western product assumes they do.

Tenant pays M-Pesa Paybill

Tenant sends rent to your existing Paybill, using their unit number as the account reference — exactly as they do today. Nothing changes for them.

You get notified in under 3 seconds

Safaricom's M-Pesa callback hits RentLoop. Payment is matched, recorded, and a WhatsApp notification is sent to you. The tenant gets an SMS receipt. All automatic.

Manage everything from WhatsApp

Type LIST to see who's paid. Type REMIND to nudge everyone who hasn't. No login. No URL. No spreadsheet. The app you already have open is the entire product.

Commands

Your entire office in WhatsApp.

Every management task available as a simple message. No menus. No UI to learn.

Landlord commands
LIST Paid vs unpaid for the current month — full unit breakdown
TOTAL Total collected vs expected, and outstanding balance
REMIND Sends an SMS reminder to every unpaid tenant instantly
RECEIPT 4B Resend the payment receipt for a specific unit
HISTORY 4B Last 3 months of payment history for a unit
MARK 4B PAID 12500 CASH Log a cash or bank payment manually to the ledger
ADD UNIT 4B John 0712… 12500 Register a new unit with tenant details and expected rent
REPLACE 4B Grace 0745… 12500 Swap to a new tenant — payment history preserved
SET RENT 4B 14000 Update expected rent — takes effect immediately
CLAIM TXN-ABC TO 4B Assign an unmatched payment to the correct unit
BULK ADD Upload all tenants at once via CSV file in WhatsApp
Agent commands
CLIENTS List all managed landlords with unit counts and status
LIST Wanjiku Paid vs unpaid for a specific landlord in your portfolio
TOTAL ALL Combined collected vs expected across all landlords
REMIND Wanjiku Send reminders for one landlord's unpaid tenants
RECEIPT Wanjiku 4B Resend a receipt for a unit under a specific landlord
